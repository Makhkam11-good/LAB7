## Code-native mark and fonts

The NOVA monogram is drawn by MarkPainter in lib/main.dart and exported at Android/iOS icon sizes by test/demo_capture_test.dart. It does not use a third-party brand. Roboto font files and their license are in assets/fonts/.

This submission includes only the four active PNG catalog images. Legacy JPGs from the earlier lab are excluded.
